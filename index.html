Demo script

The controlled fabrication demo

Use the same fictional record learners will later see in their own materials — the industrial visit note. Gold lines are exact delivery; the rest is your own guidance.

"I'm going to show you an AI-written note. Some of it is right. Some of it is wrong on purpose, so you can see how checking actually works."

Display the AI draft: "On 10 August, 35 trainees from Batch 5A visited Bansal Auto Components. The plant runs a fully automated line needing no manual checks, as confirmed by the visit register."

"Watch what I do in the next 30 seconds. I mark every fact, figure, date, name and source. Then I separate what the register actually supports from what it doesn't."

Reveal the real register live: 32 trainees, one drill bit worn, register silent on automation. Correct the figure, remove the unsupported claim, keep the source line honest.

"Notice I didn't guess. I checked, then I corrected, qualified or removed. That's the whole routine. Now you'll do the same with your own record."

Wrong-answer-in-class protocol

When the live tool surprises you

This is for an unplanned wrong answer — not the demo, which is deliberately wrong.

Pause and name it out loud"That answer doesn't look right — let's not just accept it. This is exactly the moment this course is about."
Mark it live, with the classAsk: "What in this answer needs checking? A figure, a date, a name?" Get the class to mark it, don't do it alone.
Check against a real record, or say so honestlyIf a record is on hand, verify together. If not, say plainly: "I will verify this and confirm next session" — and do it.
If learners look anxious or start distrusting all AI output: reassure that this is normal AI behaviour, not a broken tool, and it is exactly why the marking routine exists.
Turn it into evidence, not just a momentNote what happened in your facilitator log. If it affects a graded task, follow your escalation path rather than deciding alone.

Contingencies

When something doesn't go to plan

Access failure Tool or internet is down. Switch to the printed demo record and run the marking routine on paper. Learners complete the write-up when access returns.
Low confidence A learner is unsure how to mark or correct. Pair them with a batchmate who has finished one round, rather than doing it for them.
Fast finisher Give them the retry example from the Practice Bot to attempt on their own, or ask them to help a peer explain their marking.
Shared device Rotate every 10 minutes on a visible timer. The learner not on the device marks on paper and swaps in to correct.
